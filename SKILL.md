# Dhvani Rakshak - Simple Project Guide

## 1. What is Dhvani Rakshak?
Dhvani Rakshak is an AI security system that detects fake AI voices and phone scam calls in real-time to protect people from banking fraud and extortion.

---

## 2. All Features & Uses (Short & Simple)

### 📞 1. Phone Line Interceptor (Telecom Gateway)
* **What it does:** Scans incoming phone calls on telecom networks.
* **Use:** Detects fake AI voice calls during live phone calls and automatically hangs up on scammers.

### 🎙️ 2. Live Voice Scanner (Live Monitor)
* **What it does:** Real-time microphone voice scanner.
* **Use:** Listens to speech through your microphone and instantly shows if the speaker is a real human or a fake AI voice.

### ⚡ 3. Voice Fake Simulator (Call Simulator)
* **What it does:** Testing area for voice scams.
* **Use:** Lets you test pre-set AI scam samples, record your microphone, or upload audio files to see detection speed.

### 📊 4. Security Overview (Executive Dashboard)
* **What it does:** Simple security dashboard.
* **Use:** Displays total calls scanned, scam calls blocked, money saved from bank fraud, and live system stats.

### 👁️ 5. Why Is It Fake? (Explainable AI)
* **What it does:** Simple explanation of detection results.
* **Use:** Shows clear reasons why a voice was marked fake (such as robotic pitch, missing breath, or AI software patterns).

### 🛡️ 6. VIP Protection (Government & VIP Shield)
* **What it does:** Extra security mode for leaders and executives.
* **Use:** Provides ultra-strict voice checks for high-profile people to prevent deepfake impersonation attacks.

### 📜 7. Digital Voice Pass (Blockchain Certificates)
* **What it does:** Digital voice certificate generator.
* **Use:** Creates a secure digital pass for your real voice so banks can easily verify your identity.

### 🧠 8. Behavior Check (Behavioral Biometrics)
* **What it does:** Checks caller behavior patterns.
* **Use:** Spots scam red flags like late-night calls, panic pressure, and urgent money transfer demands.

### 🔒 9. Register Voice (Voice Enrollment)
* **What it does:** Saves a mathematical voice signature in 5 seconds.
* **Use:** Learns your real voice profile. **It never stores raw audio recordings**, keeping your privacy 100% safe.

### ⚙️ 10. System Settings (Policy Configuration)
* **What it does:** Risk sensitivity and auto-block controls.
* **Use:** Lets you adjust risk levels (normal vs strict) and set money transfer limits for mandatory OTP checks.

### 📑 11. Safety Reports (Compliance Audit Logs)
* **What it does:** Privacy and compliance audit records.
* **Use:** Gives downloadable proof that zero raw audio is saved and all private data stays protected.

### 📱 12. Mobile Call Interceptor (Android Floating Badge)
* **What it does:** Floating risk window during regular phone calls.
* **Use:** Displays a live risk badge directly on your phone screen during incoming calls, showing the exact risk score (e.g. Score: 94.8) and alert level (RED or GREEN).

---

## 3. How Risk Scoring Works

* **0 - 59 (GREEN Alert):** Authentic human voice. Call allowed.
* **60 - 84 (YELLOW Alert):** Suspicious voice or high-value transfer. Secondary OTP check required.
* **85 - 100 (RED Alert):** Harmful scam AI voice or fake clone. Call automatically blocked!
